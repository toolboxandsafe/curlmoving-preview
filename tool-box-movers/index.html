// 04 — FAQCommon questions.

If yours isn't here, text me — I answer every text personally.

Do I need to help with the move?

Nope. I just need access at both pickup and dropoff and I handle the rest.

Should I empty my toolbox before the move?

Leave it loaded. Lock the drawers and remove anything loose on the outside. If you have a top or side hutch, let me know if it's bolted — I'll run a strap around it either way.

When do I pay?

After the service. I accept cash, check, Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, Apple Pay, and credit card (+3.5%).

Can you do same-day pickup and delivery?

Generally yes. Sometimes I have moves in between, especially for longer-distance jobs. I finalize the schedule the day before so you know exactly what's happening on move day.

What areas do you service?

Based in Gilbert, serving all of Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa, Chandler, Tempe, and surrounding areas. Outside Phoenix metro is fine — just ask.

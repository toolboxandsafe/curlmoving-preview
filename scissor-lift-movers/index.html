// 01 — MethodHow I move
your lift.

Right trailer, right tie-down, right route. Platform stowed, wheels chocked, nothing shifting on the freeway.

  • Drive-on loading — The drop deck sits low, so the lift rolls on under its own power when it runs. No crane, no drama.
  • Dead unit? I winch it — Flat battery, blown hydraulic, seized wheel motor — tell me up front and I bring the gear to pull it on.
  • Chained at the tie points — Four-point tie-down at the manufacturer's designated points, plus wheel chocks. Nothing rides on the guardrails.
  • Platform down and stowed — Deck lowered, rails folded or pinned per the model, potholes protection retracted before we roll.
  • Route planned for height and weight — Loaded deck height, gate widths, and low branches get checked before move day, not at the gate.
  • Fully insured — Your lift is covered from the moment it touches my deck.
Before I arrive Have the keys on site and the unit charged or fueled enough to drive on and off. Lower the platform and retract the outriggers if it has them. Know the make, model, and weight — it's on the data plate near the controls. Clear a straight approach on firm ground, and flag any soft dirt, gravel, tight gates, or overhead wires so I bring the right setup.

// 04 — FAQCommon questions.

If yours isn't here, text me — I answer every text personally.

Do I need to be there for the move?

Not necessarily. I need access at both ends and the keys available at pickup. Plenty of job site and rental yard moves happen with nobody but the gate code.

What do you need to quote it?

Make, model, and platform height — or just a photo of the data plate near the controls. Then both addresses, whether it runs, and what the ground is like at each end. That's enough for a firm number.

My lift won't start. Can you still move it?

Yes, but tell me before move day. A dead unit gets winched on instead of driven on, which changes the equipment and the time on site. It's not a problem — it's just a different plan.

Can you move rough terrain lifts?

Send me the weight off the data plate and I'll tell you straight whether it's a fit for my trailer or whether you need a heavier hauler. I'd rather turn a job down than show up with the wrong rig.

When do I pay?

After the service. I accept cash, check, Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, Apple Pay, and credit card (+3.5%).

What areas do you service?

Based in Gilbert, serving Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa, Chandler, Tempe, and surrounding areas. Outside Phoenix metro is fine — just ask.

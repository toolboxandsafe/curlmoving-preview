// 04 — ConsiderationsScottsdale-specific notes.

A few realities of Scottsdale's gated communities, custom homes, and seasonal residents.

Gated communities & guard gates

Most Scottsdale luxury communities have staffed guard gates. Add me to your visitor list or provide a gate code in advance. Some communities (DC Ranch, Estancia) require contractor registration — let me know if this applies.

Custom home layouts

Scottsdale custom homes often have unique floor plans. If you have a dedicated safe room, wine cellar conversion, or basement installation, send me photos of the path so I can bring the right equipment.

Snowbird & second homes

Many Scottsdale homeowners split time between states. If you're coordinating a move while out of town, I can work with your property manager or house sitter. Just make sure someone's there to verify final placement.

Dealer deliveries

I partner with Scottsdale-area safe dealers for white-glove delivery. If you're purchasing from a local showroom and need professional installation, the dealer may already have my number.

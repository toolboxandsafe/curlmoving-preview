// 02 — Before the moveWhat you need
to do first.

A few rules of physics: drink machines fully loaded are a death trap. Chocolate at 110°F is a tragedy. Read these.

Drink machines — must be unloaded Empty all bottles and cans. I won't move a fully-loaded drink machine. They're too heavy and the risk of damage to the machine, the property, or me is real.
Snack machines — leave loaded if you want Don't have to empty. But chocolates may melt during transport on hot Arizona days. If your route is summer afternoon, consider pulling the chocolate.

// 04 — FAQCommon questions.

If yours isn't here, text me — I answer every text personally.

Do I need to unload before moving?

Drink machines: yes — fully unload, or I won't move it. Snack machines: no, but chocolates may melt in summer.

Do I have to be at pickup or drop off?

If I have to move it through a single door, yes. Double doors with a removable middle piece — no. I always notify 30 minutes before arriving at the pickup location.

Does my machine go straight to drop off after pickup?

Sometimes I have other pickups in between, especially on longer routes. I know the day-before schedule by afternoon and will communicate it with you.

How do I pay?

Usually after the service, but you can pay before. I accept cash, check, Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, Apple Pay, and credit card (+3.5%).

Gilbert is consistently ranked among the best places to raise a family in Arizona. That changes what I move and why.

Three generations, one family I've moved safes between three generations of the same Gilbert family — grandparents downsizing, parents upgrading, kids buying their first home. It's that kind of community.

What family-focused Gilbert means for safe ownership:

  • Firearms + children = mandatory safe storage. Responsible Gilbert gun owners prioritize keeping firearms secured.
  • Document protection — Families with kids have important documents — birth certificates, passports, estate papers. Fireproof safes protect what can't be replaced.
  • Inheritance moves — Multi-generational families sometimes transfer safes between homes when parents downsize or kids buy their first home.
  • Long-term installations — Gilbert's high homeownership means safes get installed once and stay put. Worth doing right.

// 04 — ConsiderationsGilbert-specific notes.

A few local realities that shape Gilbert moves.

HOA rules

Most Gilbert communities have active HOAs. Some have restrictions on delivery vehicles or working hours. Let me know if your HOA requires advance notice or has specific rules about work trucks.

Garage-based living

Gilbert families use their garages — workshops, gyms, storage. If your safe is going in a finished garage space, measure the clearance. I've seen garages with gym equipment that need to be rearranged before I can position the safe.

New construction

Gilbert is still growing, especially in Southeast Gilbert and toward Queen Creek. For new builds, coordinate safe delivery after your final walkthrough and flooring installation.

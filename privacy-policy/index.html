How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Introduction

Curl Moving ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services.

Information we collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information — Name, phone number, email address, and physical addresses (for pickup and delivery).
  • Service information — Details about the items you need moved, including photos, dimensions, and weight.
  • Communication records — Text messages, calls, and emails related to your service.

How we use your information

  • Provide quotes and schedule moving services
  • Communicate with you about your move
  • Process payments
  • Improve our services

Information sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only:

  • With service providers who assist in our operations (payment processors)
  • With the services described under “The quote form” below, which are used to receive your request and schedule your job
  • When required by law
  • To protect our rights or safety

The quote form

When you submit the quote form on this site, your answers are delivered directly to Ryan’s email inbox. They are not handled by any third-party form service.

  • Cloudflare hosts this website and processes the form submission on its way to us. Cloudflare already carries all traffic to this site.
  • Google Workspace hosts the email inbox the request arrives in.
  • Trello and RingCentral are used to schedule your job and to text or call you back about it.

The pickup and delivery address fields on the form are optional. You never have to enter them to request a quote — we are happy to get those details over the phone instead. Anything you do enter is used only to prepare your quote and carry out your move.

Form submissions are not stored in any public database and are not used for advertising or marketing lists.

Data security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information. Form submissions are encrypted in transit. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Your rights

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information

Mobile / SMS information sharing policy

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or any information collected through SMS opt-in with third parties or affiliates for promotional or marketing purposes. Your mobile information will only be used by Curl Moving to provide you with service-related communications as described in our SMS Terms of Service below.

  • Your consent to receive SMS messages is not shared with any third party.
  • Your mobile phone number will not be sold to or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • Any information you provide via SMS will not be disclosed to third parties except as required to provide our moving services to you directly (for example, communicating with our drivers for your scheduled move).

SMS terms of service

By opting into SMS from a web form, phone call, or other medium, you are agreeing to receive SMS messages from Curl Moving.

Types of messages you may receive

  • Appointment reminders and move scheduling confirmations
  • Order alerts and delivery status updates
  • Quote confirmations and pricing notifications
  • Account notifications
  • Customer service communications

Important information

  • Message frequency varies based on your service requests and account activity
  • Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan
  • Curl Moving does not charge for SMS messages, but standard carrier rates apply

Opt-out instructions

  • To opt out at any time, reply STOP to any message
  • You will receive a confirmation message and no further SMS messages will be sent
  • You may also contact us directly to opt out
  • For assistance, reply HELP to any message

Carrier disclaimer

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Mobile information sharing

Your mobile opt-in consent, phone number, and any personal information obtained through our SMS program will not be shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes.

Links to other sites

Our service may contain links to other sites. If you click a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. These external sites are not operated by us, so we strongly advise you to review their privacy policies. We have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

Children’s privacy

Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we delete it from our servers immediately. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take the necessary action.

Cookies

This site uses cookies for analytics and advertising measurement. Our Cookie Policy lists each one, what it does, and how to turn it off.

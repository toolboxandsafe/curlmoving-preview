Cookie
policy.

Which cookies this site sets, what they do, and how to turn them off.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. They let a site remember things between page loads — for example, that you have visited before. This policy covers cookies and similar technologies used on curlmoving.com. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle information you give us directly.

What this site actually uses

We keep this short because there is not much to report. This site has no login, no shopping cart, and no comment system, so it sets no cookies of its own. Every cookie described below is set by Google.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit, which pages they read, and which pages lead to a phone call or text. This tells us which services people actually need.

  • _ga — distinguishes one visitor from another. Expires after 2 years.
  • _ga_<ID> — keeps track of a single visit (a "session"). Expires after 2 years.

Advertising

We advertise on Google. These cookies let Google tell us when an ad click led to someone calling or texting us, so we are not paying for ads that do not work.

  • _gcl_au — records that you arrived from a Google ad, so a resulting call can be attributed to it. Expires after 90 days.
  • Google advertising cookies — set by Google on its own domains, these may be used to show you our ads again elsewhere.

Fonts

Our typefaces load from Google Fonts. This sets no cookies, but Google receives your IP address as part of serving the font files.

Tracking phone and text clicks

When you tap a phone number or text link on this site, we record that a tap happened, which page it came from, and which part of the page. We do not record your phone number, the content of your message, or anything you say on the call. The call itself is not recorded or monitored.

Why you are not seeing a cookie banner

Curl Moving is a local moving company operating in Arizona and serving customers in the Phoenix metro area. We are not subject to the laws that require a consent banner before setting analytics cookies. Rather than show a banner that would not mean anything, we have written this page and listed the controls below.

How to turn these off

  • In your browser — Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge all let you block or delete cookies in their privacy settings. Blocking them will not affect anything on this site, because nothing here depends on them.
  • Google Analytics — install Google's official opt-out browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
  • Google ad personalisation — manage it at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Private browsing — an incognito or private window discards all cookies when you close it.

Do Not Track

Browsers can send a "Do Not Track" signal. There is still no agreed standard for how sites should respond to it, so this site does not change its behaviour based on that signal. The controls listed above do work.

Links to other sites

Our pages link out to Instagram, Facebook and Google. Those links set no cookies until you click them. Once you are on another company's site, that company's cookie policy applies, not ours.

Changes to this policy

If we add or remove a tool that uses cookies, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
